# ToneVault – Owner Dashboard Business Decisions

## 1. Total Orders

**Metric:** Number of saved marketplace orders.

**Actual result:** 5 orders.

**Business interpretation:** Total orders indicate the level of purchasing activity on ToneVault. An increasing number of orders may suggest greater marketplace activity and buyer engagement.

**Owner decision:** Monitor order growth and determine whether buyer acquisition and seller participation need improvement.

## 2. Total Order Value

**Metric:** Combined gross value of all saved buyer orders.

**Actual result:** ₱1,663,000.00 across 5 orders.

**Business interpretation:** Total order value represents the combined value of buyer transactions recorded in the marketplace. It indicates the monetary volume of transactions, but it is not the same as revenue earned by ToneVault.

**Owner decision:** Monitor the total value of saved orders to assess marketplace transaction activity and identify opportunities to increase buyer spending.

## 3. Average Order Value

**Metric:** Total order value divided by the number of saved orders.

**Actual result:** ₱332,600.00.

**Business interpretation:** Average order value indicates how much customers spend per transaction in ToneVault's premium pre-owned guitar marketplace.

**Owner decision:** Monitor changes in AOV to understand purchasing behavior and evaluate whether ToneVault is attracting buyers within its intended premium market.

## 4. Top-Selling Guitars

**Metric:** Guitar listings with the highest total quantity sold.

**Actual result:** Five-way tie, with 1 unit sold each:
- Fender Jazzmaster
- Fender Stratocaster
- Fender Telecaster
- Gibson SG Standard
- PRS Custom 24

**Business interpretation:** These five guitars currently share the highest recorded sales volume. Since each has only one recorded sale, there is not yet enough transaction data to identify a single best-selling model.

**Owner decision:** Monitor sales trends as more orders are recorded. Use these results as an initial reference when deciding which models to feature, source, or promote.

## 5. Low-Stock Listings

**Metric:** Available guitar listings with stock at or below the current threshold of 2 units.

**Actual result:** 7 low-stock listings.

**Business interpretation:** These listings have limited available inventory and may soon become unavailable for purchase.

**Owner decision:** Monitor low-stock listings and contact sellers to encourage inventory replenishment where possible.

## Additional Metric Note: Platform Revenue

**Metric:** Revenue earned by ToneVault through seller commissions and verification services.

**Revenue model:**
- 8% seller commission.
- ₱2,800 remote verification fee.
- ₱8,500 physical verification fee.

**Actual result:** Pending database integration.

**Business interpretation:** Platform revenue measures the income ToneVault actually earns from commissions and optional verification services. It cannot be calculated from total order value alone.

**Owner decision:** Integrate and track commission and verification-fee records before using this metric to evaluate the platform's revenue performance.
